# The Way Out

## Project Description

**The Way Out** is a single-player puzzle and adventure game developed for the PS01: GlitchVerse – The World Is Broken challenge.

The game takes place in a corrupted virtual world where reality is breaking apart. Players must explore mysterious environments, solve interactive puzzles, and uncover hidden clues to escape. The game combines exploration, logical reasoning, and challenging puzzles to create an engaging adventure.

## Storyline

After completing his academic submissions, a frustrated student sits down at his laptop to play his favorite game. Instead, he encounters a glitched screen and a mysterious voice coming from within it.

The voice belongs to the game's developer, who is trapped inside his own creation. He asks the student for help, claiming that he is the chosen one capable of saving him.

Before the student can understand what is happening, he is pulled into the game.

When he opens his eyes, he finds himself in a strange, fog-covered virtual world with a single door ahead. A floating manuscript beside the door provides instructions for the level he is about to enter.

With no way back, the student begins his journey to find the trapped developer and escape the broken world.

## Level 1 – The Abandoned Building

The first level takes place inside an abandoned building containing seven interconnected rooms. Each of the first six rooms has a hidden numerical code concealed among scattered furniture and misplaced objects.

The player must explore each room, discover the clues, and enter the correct code to unlock the next door. However, three randomly selected rooms contain a specially highlighted digit.

These three digits form the final combination required to unlock the seventh room. The player has four attempts to guess the correct arrangement of the digits. If all attempts are exhausted, the player must restart the level from the first room.

The objective is to solve the puzzles, unlock the final room, and find the way forward into the next stage of the game.
